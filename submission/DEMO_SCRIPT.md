# ASTRAL Crew — demo narration (research lead)

“ASTRAL helps an astronaut notice when today’s health check differs from their own recent pattern. This example is simulated: ASTRAL flags the change, asks the astronaut to verify it, and points them to the approved mission procedure. In a separate research sandbox, our WESAD model runs on a clearly synthetic 60-second wrist-feature example; its uncalibrated score is not a diagnosis or astronaut stress state. Biology Evidence shows real NASA Inspiration4 serum samples from four coded crew members across seven pre- and post-flight timepoints. Those samples do not validate our software. ASTRAL is an offline, non-diagnostic prototype.”

About 30 seconds at a clear speaking pace. This narration is for a short video option; the slide deck contains exactly seven slides, including the title slide.
