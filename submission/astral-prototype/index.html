ASTRAL RESPONSE

A simple review sequence

The system supports the astronaut's decision; it does not make a diagnosis.

01

Check the measurement.Pause, follow the device instructions, and repeat a value that seems unexpected.

02

Review the pattern.ASTRAL SENSE and INTELLIGENCE show changes against your own history; the method is visible and reproducible.

03

Check the context.ASTRAL ENVIRONMENT keeps mission conditions separate from personal measurements. A relationship is not proof of cause.

04

Use the mission protocol.For persistent changes or concerning symptoms, follow the onboard checklist and contact the flight surgeon when possible.

NASA Human Research Program

Five hazards. One clear check-in.

ASTRAL uses NASA's human-spaceflight hazard framework to explain why daily indicators and context matter. The cards are educational links, not a NASA-approved operational protocol.

FRAMEWORK 5 HAZARDS
☼
CONTEXT

Space radiation

Radiation exposure is a mission hazard. The prototype can display a dose value supplied by a validated monitor, but no monitor is connected here.

Potential context: exposure history · dose trend
◌
SELF-REPORT

Isolation & confinement

Confinement can affect behavioral health. A private mood and sleep check helps the astronaut notice a personal trend without labeling a psychological condition.

Indicators: sleep · self-reported mood · symptoms
⇢
DELAY CONTEXT

Distance from Earth

Communication delays make immediate consultation unavailable. Offline checklists and a clear log can support the crew while they follow mission procedures.

Support: local records · onboard procedures
⌂
TREND

Gravity fields

Altered gravity can affect the body. The prototype tracks simple trends such as resting heart rate and sleep; it does not measure bone density.

Indicators: cardiovascular trend · sleep
▣
SENSOR INPUT

Hostile / closed environment

Environmental conditions should be displayed separately from personal health. A change in a cabin sensor is context to check—not a diagnosis of an astronaut.

Context: pressure · cabin gas · temperature
＋
ACTION

From signal to next step

Repeat an unexpected measurement, compare it with personal history, record symptoms, and follow the approved mission checklist if concern persists.

ASTRAL role: organize · inform · guide

Study design and boundaries
What this NASA dataset can support

Inspiration4 was a three-day low Earth orbit mission with four crew members. This CMP file contains seven serum collection times: L−92, L−44, L−3, R+1, R+45, R+82, and R+194. The samples are pre-flight and post-flight, not continuous in-flight monitoring.

Prototype limits
Visible limits are part of responsible health software.
01

No clinical validation.The rolling median / MAD pattern rule is illustrative and not clinically validated.

02

No diagnosis or causal inference.Signals prompt verification and approved procedures.

03

No live sensor feed.OSD-575 measurements appear only in the separate Biology view; daily check-in and habitat demo values are synthetic.

04

No safety-critical control.The software does not operate lighting, spacecraft systems, or task assignments.

05

Separate research-model sandbox.This browser includes the WESAD model weights and a synthetic feature-vector demo. It does not read daily check-ins or live signals; laboratory results do not establish astronaut validity.
